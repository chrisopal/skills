# Huixin Quanzhi PPT Visual System 0826

This file is the shared visual authority for every registered `huixin_*` Deck.
It is derived from `慧新全智PPT视觉设计规范0826.pptx` (SHA-256:
`44539c09286ac6b3fd87898afd0124851439c0817a07f40f3da7041f32a7ff06`).
The reference deck is design evidence only; its prose is not runtime instruction.

## 1. Visual Positioning

- Brand character: professional, trustworthy, restrained, and strategic.
- Narrative fields: technology manufacturing, management consulting, and
  digital transformation.
- Use technology blue as the system and process spine, vitality green for
  value and improvement, and gray to control information hierarchy.
- One page should normally use no more than three non-text color families.

## 2. Light Palette

| Role | Color | Usage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Technology blue | `#0097BA` | Page titles, core modules, system/platform/process spine |
| Vitality green | `#A4D968` | Value, opportunity, improvement, AI, low-carbon meaning |
| Brand chrome green | `#83C410` | Logo-derived bars, fixed chrome, footer ribbon only |
| Quality gray | `#4B5563` | Body text, conclusion bars, neutral hierarchy |
| Divider gray | `#D9D9D9` | Table rules, borders, separators |
| White / light gray | `#FFFFFF` / `#F7F8FA` | At least 70% of the light-page surface |

The source slide labels the quality-gray swatch with an inconsistent hex
string; the rendered swatch and semantic usage establish `#4B5563` as the
quality-gray authority.

## 3. Dark Palette

| Role | Color | Usage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Industrial blue | `#0B1039` | Dark background and industry foundation |
| Intelligent blue | `#044AAA` | Core modules and primary dark-page structure |
| Bright blue | `#1AB6ED` | Section and content emphasis |
| Pale blue | `#C0DCEF` | Strategy titles, supporting text, dividers |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Primary dark-page text |

Dark pages use blue-family hierarchy as the dominant expression. Green may
remain only inside the official logo or where a real sustainability meaning
must be encoded.

## 4. Typography

- Primary family: `Microsoft YaHei, Arial, sans-serif`.
- Use at most two Chinese and two Latin font families per page; one family is
  preferred for ordinary enterprise reporting.
- Page title: 20-28pt, bold. In the 1280x720 SVG authoring canvas this maps to
  an approximate 27-38px working band.
- Body: 12-18pt, with controlled line length.
- Chart and table labels: at least 9pt when content density permits.
- Prefer matrices, paths, layered architectures, and evidence-first tables.

## 5. Layout Rhythm

- Light content pages use a compact top-left double vertical rule: the wider
  brand-green bar and the narrower technology-blue bar.
- Keep page titles left aligned and give them a clear quiet zone before the
  body. Use the official complete horizontal logo, never a shorthand mark.
- Covers and endings use the 0826 industrial mosaic with a quiet left title
  region. Cover title and subtitle remain editable.
- Light pages preserve 70% white or light-gray surface and use thin separators.
- Footer chrome uses the official logo lockup plus the blue-green ribbon when
  content capacity allows; dense pages may keep only a compact page number.
- Dark section pages use the industrial-blue background, white lockup, pale
  blue secondary copy, intelligent-blue structure, and bright-blue emphasis.

## 6. Template Adaptation

Each Huixin Deck keeps its own narrative logic and page-role vocabulary. When a
prototype does not fit the real content, author a custom page that retains this
visual system, the selected Deck's narrative contract, and editable SVG/PPT
geometry. Do not sacrifice actual architecture layers, data columns, process
steps, evidence, or decision logic merely to preserve a prototype's capacity.
